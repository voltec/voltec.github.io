Couchdial Privacy Policy

Effective October 27, 2026

The short version

  • Couchdial has no accounts and no servers of its own. Your playlists, logins and viewing history never reach us.
  • If you use iCloud, your sources move between your devices through your private iCloud. Everything the app stores there is end-to-end encrypted.
  • No ads, no tracking, no analytics and no crash-reporting services.
  • The app connects only to the servers in the sources you add, and to Apple.

Who is responsible

Couchdial is made by Mikhail Mukminov, an individual developer ("we"). For the purposes of the GDPR and the UK GDPR, we are the controller for the Couchdial apps. Contact: voltec.apps@gmail.com.

This policy covers Couchdial for iPhone, iPad, Apple TV and Mac, and this website. The website uses no cookies, scripts or third-party resources.

How Couchdial works

Couchdial is a player for your own M3U playlists and Xtream accounts. It contains no channels. What you add is stored on your devices and, if you're signed in to iCloud, in your private iCloud storage, which Apple runs for you. We don't run any server that your data passes through, and we can't see what you store in your iCloud.

What the app stores and where

Source details. The name you give the source, its type (M3U link, M3U file or Xtream), the server address (host and port only), the Xtream user name, your settings for the source (for example "Show on Apple TV"), and the expiry date and connection limit if your provider reports them. Stored on your device and, if you use iCloud, in your private iCloud database, end-to-end encrypted. Apple sees only technical metadata, such as the record type and dates. Used to show your sources and move them between your devices.

Links, logins and playlist files. The full playlist link (it may contain a token), the Xtream server, user name and password, and the contents of an M3U file you import. Stored in the Keychain on each device. In your private iCloud only end-to-end encrypted: links and logins in encrypted fields, playlist files encrypted with their own key before upload. The keys are kept in your iCloud Keychain, so neither Apple nor we can read them.

Your devices. For each device where Couchdial runs: its name, its type and when the app was last open on it. When you send a source to a specific device, a record of which source went where, the name of the sending device and when it arrived. Stored in your private iCloud database, end-to-end encrypted, to show "Apple TV nearby", send a source to that device and confirm that it arrived.

Favorites, recent channels, Order changes, player settings, parental PIN. Only on this device.

Channel lists and programme guide. Downloaded from your sources and kept in a cache on your device.

Purchases. Apple handles payment. The app asks Apple's StoreKit on your device whether you have Couchdial Pro. We never see your name, Apple Account or payment details. Apple gives us sales reports that don't identify you.

Sending a source to Apple TV with a QR code

The Apple TV shows a QR code with a one-time code and an encryption key. Your iPhone scans it, encrypts the source with that key (AES-GCM, 256-bit) and leaves the encrypted package in the app's public iCloud database under the one-time code. The Apple TV downloads the package, decrypts it and confirms, and your iPhone then deletes the package. The key exists only in the QR code: without it nobody, including Apple and us, can read the package. Each code works once and only for 10 minutes. If the Apple TV never confirms, the encrypted package can stay in the public database, still unreadable without the key.

Who the app connects to

  • The servers in your sources: playlists, Xtream servers, streams, and logo and guide addresses listed in your playlist. Like any website, they see your IP address and what the app requests. Their own privacy policies apply, and we get nothing from them. Addresses that start with http:// are not encrypted.
  • Apple: iCloud (syncing and QR transfer) and the App Store (purchases).

Nothing else. We have no servers and keep no logs.

What we don't do

  • No ads, no tracking, and no analytics or crash-reporting services in the app. The app never asks for permission to track you.
  • We don't sell or share personal information, and we don't build profiles.
  • Sorting your playlist ("Order") happens on your device.

If you turned on "Share with App Developers" in your device's privacy settings, Apple may share crash reports and aggregated usage statistics with us. That is Apple's feature, under Apple's privacy policy, and the reports don't identify you.

When you email us

We receive your email address and what you write, use it only to answer you, and keep it only as long as we need to help you, unless the law requires longer. Please don't send passwords or full playlist links.

Legal bases (GDPR and UK GDPR)

  • Storing and syncing your sources, playing streams and checking your purchase are needed to provide the app you chose to use (Art. 6(1)(b)).
  • Answering your emails: our legitimate interest in helping you (Art. 6(1)(f)), or performance of a contract when it's about your purchase.

How long data is kept

  • On your device: until you delete the source, clear recent channels or delete the app.
  • In your iCloud: until you delete the source on an iPhone, iPad or Mac, which removes it from all your devices. Deleting a source on Apple TV only hides it there.
  • With us: nothing from the app, because nothing from the app reaches us.

Your rights

Under the GDPR and the UK GDPR you can access, correct, delete, restrict or object to processing of your data, receive it in a portable format and withdraw consent. You can also complain to a data protection authority. The app's data is on your devices and in your iCloud, so you control it directly: edit or delete sources in the app, or delete Couchdial's iCloud data (see Support). Apple lets you request a copy of your iCloud data at privacy.apple.com. For anything else, email voltec.apps@gmail.com. We reply within one month.

California and other US states: we don't sell or share personal information, don't use it for targeted advertising and don't use sensitive personal information.

Children

Couchdial is not made for children. We don't knowingly collect personal data from anyone, including children.

Security

Logins are kept in the Keychain. Everything the app stores in your iCloud is end-to-end encrypted, and the QR transfer is encrypted with a one-time key. Keep your devices up to date, use two-factor authentication for your Apple Account, and prefer sources with https:// addresses.

Changes

When we change this policy, we update this page and the date above. If the app starts handling your data in a new way, we'll describe it here before that update is released and ask for your consent in the app where it's required.
