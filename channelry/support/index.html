Channelry Support

Channelry is a player for your own IPTV playlists on iPhone, iPad, Apple TV and Mac. It doesn't include, sell or recommend channels, playlists or providers.

Contact: voltec.apps@gmail.com. Please tell us your device and system version, the app version (Settings → About) and what you did and saw; a screenshot helps. Never send passwords or full playlist links.

Frequently asked questions

How do I add a playlist?
On iPhone, iPad or Mac tap Add source and choose Link (an M3U playlist link), Xtream (server, login and password) or File (an M3U file). The app checks the source, imports it and sorts the channels into categories. On Apple TV choose Add from iPhone, Scan a code or Type on this Apple TV.

Why won't a channel play?
Channelry plays what your source sends and shows the reason when a channel fails. On the error screen tap What to tell the provider to copy a ready-made message.

  • Access denied (401, 403): the login is wrong, the subscription doesn't include this channel, or the provider blocks your network or region.
  • Not found (404): the channel moved or was removed. Refresh the source; if it's still broken, the provider needs to fix the link.
  • Server error or no response: the provider's server is down or overloaded. Try later or another mirror of the channel.
  • Subscription expired, or connection limit reached: reported by your provider.
  • A web page instead of a stream: ask the provider for a direct stream or M3U link.
  • Unsupported format: turn on Compatible player for other formats in Settings → Player.

How do I move my sources to Apple TV?
With the same Apple Account, sign in to iCloud on both devices and sources with Show on Apple TV appear by themselves. With any Apple Account, choose Scan a code on Apple TV and scan it with your iPhone camera. When Channelry is open on your Apple TV, your iPhone also offers Send to Apple TV.

How do I cancel my subscription?
Subscriptions are managed by Apple: on iPhone or iPad open Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions; on Apple TV Settings → Users and Accounts → your account → Subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before the period or free trial ends. Deleting the app doesn't cancel a subscription. See Apple's guide. Refunds: reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I restore my purchase?
Sign in with the Apple Account you used to buy Pro and open Settings → Purchases → Restore purchase. Pro works on every device with that Apple Account and for family members through Family Sharing.

How do I delete my data?
Delete a source on iPhone, iPad or Mac to remove it from all devices and iCloud. Delete the app to remove its local data and Keychain logins. To remove everything from iCloud, open Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Storage, choose Channelry and delete its data. We keep none of your data.
