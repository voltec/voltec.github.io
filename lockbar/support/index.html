Lockbar Support

Lockbar finds photos of passports and IDs, driver's licenses, bank cards, passwords and recovery codes, seed phrases and other documents in your photo library, and locks them in an encrypted vault on your iPhone. Everything runs on the device: no account, no servers.

Contact: voltec366+lockbar@gmail.com. We usually reply within 2 business days. Please never send us photos of your documents, your PIN or your recovery key.

Getting started

  1. Open the app and tap Allow access to Photos. Full access lets the app check your whole library; with limited access it checks only the photos you selected.
  2. The check runs on your iPhone and shows what it found by category.
  3. Open a category and review the photos (everything found is preselected; uncheck false alarms, or switch to Cards to go one by one), then tap Hide.
  4. The first time you hide something you create a 6-digit PIN and can turn on Face ID.
  5. The app encrypts each photo and checks the copy, then iOS asks you to confirm deleting the originals. They stay in Recently Deleted in Photos for about 30 days.

Frequently asked questions

Is anything uploaded?
No. Photos, recognized text, results and the vault stay on your device. The app has no servers and no analytics. Purchases go through Apple.

Why didn't it find a photo?
Detection is not perfect. Printed documents and screenshots are found best. Handwritten notes (for example a seed phrase on paper) are often missed by the normal check. Turn on the deep scan in Settings for a slower, more thorough check that runs while your iPhone charges. Always review the results yourself.

Why is "Intimate photos" unavailable?
This category uses Apple's on-device Sensitive Content Analysis, which only works when you turn on Sensitive Content Warning in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security. The app cannot turn it on for you.

The scan stopped when I left the app.
iOS may pause long checks. The app shows "Scan · stopped" with Continue, and nothing already checked is lost.

What is free and what is Pro?
The scan of your whole library is free. Findings in photos from the last 30 days are free to review and hide, as many as you like. Copies of the same photo are cleaned up for free. Findings in older photos show up as a count and open with Pro. You can also add your own photos and files: free for photos from the last 30 days and up to 10 files, with Pro any age and any number. Export is always free. Pro is a one-time purchase or a yearly subscription that also checks new photos automatically (prices are shown in the app).

How do I cancel or manage my subscription?
Open iOS Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions, or the App Store → your profile → Subscriptions. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

I bought Pro but the app shows Free.
Open Settings → Restore Purchases in the app, signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy.

Your vault and backups (please read)

  • The vault lives only on this iPhone. It is not included in iCloud backups, iPhone backups or Quick Start transfers to a new iPhone. To move it or keep it safe, create a backup file in Settings and store it in Files, on a computer or on a drive.
  • Keep your recovery key. Backups open only with it. Write it down or store it in a password manager, not in Photos.
  • Restore on a new iPhone: install the app, tap I have a backup (or Settings → Restore from file), choose the file and enter the recovery key.
  • Forgot your PIN? No one, including us, can open the vault without it. If you have a backup file and your recovery key, delete and reinstall the app, then restore from the backup.
  • Deleting the app deletes the vault. Make a backup file first.

Adding items yourself

In the Vault tab tap Add → From Photos or From Files. From any app, use Share → Lockbar: shared items are encrypted right away and move into the vault the next time you unlock it.

Security at a glance

  • AES-256-GCM encryption on the device
  • 6-digit PIN with optional Face ID; repeated wrong PINs add a growing delay
  • The vault locks when you leave the app (adjustable in Settings) and is covered in the app switcher
